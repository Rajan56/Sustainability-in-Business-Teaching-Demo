# Sustainability in Business: a course I would teach

A planned master's course design by **Rajan Kumar V K, D.Sc. (Tech.)**, showing how I would teach sustainable business: the course frame, a teaching approach built on open questions and evidence that disagrees, three interactive exercises, teaching and assessment in the age of AI, and master's thesis supervision.

**Live page:** https://rajan56.github.io/Sustainability-in-Business-Teaching-Demo/

## Exercises

| Exercise | Question for students |
|---|---|
| Is there a business case? | When do profit and environmental gain move together, and when do they pull apart? |
| Where the data stops | Does the EU ceiling on supplier data requests protect suppliers or blind buyers? |
| Symbolic or substantive? | How can we tell a change in practice from a change in paperwork? |

All companies and figures are fictional teaching data. The models are simplified on purpose so that students can see which assumption drives each result.

## Run locally

No build step. Open index.html, or serve the folder with python -m http.server 8000.
